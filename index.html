Privacy Policy

IPTV Player · last updated October 4, 2026

1. Summary

IPTV Player (“the app”) is a media player for live TV streams. You add your own M3U/M3U8 playlists, or load a bundled list of free public channels published by their broadcasters. The app is published by Cool App Helpdesk.

  • The developer collects no personal data. There are no user accounts and no developer-operated servers.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting, social or attribution SDKs.
  • Your playlists, favorites and viewing history stay on your device.
  • The app provides no TV content of its own. Streams come directly from the servers named in the playlists you add.

2. Data we collect

None. The developer does not receive personal data, device identifiers, IP addresses, usage analytics or the list of channels you watch. Nothing you do in the app is reported to us.

5. Network activity and third parties

IPTV Player connects only to:

  • Playlist URLs you add — fetched when you import a playlist and, if auto-refresh is on, periodically afterwards (default every 24 hours; you can change or turn this off in Settings).
  • Stream URLs — the servers listed in your playlists, or the broadcasters of the bundled free channels. The app also makes a short request to a stream to check whether it is online. These servers see your IP address like any website does; their content and privacy practices are controlled by them, not by the developer.
  • Channel logo images — loaded from the image URLs given in the playlist.

The app makes no other network connections. Its libraries (Media3 ExoPlayer, OkHttp, Coil, Room, WorkManager) work only within the scope above and do not send data to the developer or to third parties.

Content. IPTV Player is a player only. It does not host, sell or link to any channel. You are responsible for making sure you have the right to watch the streams in the playlists you add. The bundled channels are free, publicly available streams published by their broadcasters.

6. Children’s privacy

IPTV Player is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from any user, whatever their age, and contains no ads or in-app purchases.

7. Data retention and deletion

The developer holds no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side. To remove everything the app stored, use Settings → Reset all data in the app, clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → IPTV Player → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall it.

8. Security

All app data lives in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s application sandbox. Many IPTV providers only offer plain HTTP streams, so the app allows unencrypted connections to the servers you add; use HTTPS playlist and stream URLs where your provider offers them. The app is distributed through Google Play, which verifies the developer signing key on every update.

9. Changes to this policy

If any material aspect of how the app handles data changes, this page will be updated and the “last updated” date above will change.
